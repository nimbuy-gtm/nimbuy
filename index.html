Straight answers.

What changes after the optimize?

Security reviewers find their answers on the listing instead of emailing you. Procurement can see what one unit buys. AI search can tell who the product is for and what it does. Re-run the audit here and you see the new score.

What do you need from us?

Your listing URL, product website, pricing, and a one-pager or deck if you have one. Anything you can prove, like certifications, integrations or customers, goes into the listing. Anything you can't prove stays out.

Do you need access to our AWS account?

Not for the optimize: you paste the new copy yourself. For a launch, you choose: grant temporary IAM access that you revoke once the listing is live, or every field is handed to your team ready to enter.

Who builds the SaaS integration?

Your engineers. AWS requires SaaS products to connect for sign-up and billing, and that is code in your product. Nimbuy handles the listing, the pricing setup and the AWS approval.

How is the benchmark built?

Live AWS Marketplace listings are read through the AWS Marketplace Discovery API, capped at two per seller so large sellers cannot skew it, and scored with this same audit. Only aggregate numbers are published, never a single listing's score.

Why not let AI rewrite the listing?

AI drafts words. It doesn't know which reviewer questions your listing leaves open, or which claims you can prove. Every line is written from your facts only.
