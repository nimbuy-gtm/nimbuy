Privacy and legal

Last updated 2 October 2026

The short version

  • The free audit runs entirely in your browser. The listing text you paste is never sent to Nimbuy and never stored.
  • This site sets no cookies, loads no analytics and runs no advertising trackers.
  • Nimbuy receives your details only when you book a call or send an email.
  • Nothing is sold or shared for marketing.

Who is responsible

Nimbuy, founded by Krishna Kumar T S. Contact nimbuygtm@gmail.com for any question about your data, including deletion.

What is collected, and why

If you book a call. Booking is handled by Cal.com, which collects your name, email, the time you pick and anything you type into the booking form. The event is written to a Google Calendar and a Google Meet link is created. This is used only to hold the meeting.

If you email. Whatever you put in the message reaches a Google Workspace inbox, and is used only to reply and to scope work.

When you visit. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which records standard server log data such as your IP address and the pages requested. Nimbuy does not read or analyse those logs.

The free audit. The scoring runs in JavaScript on your own device. No listing text leaves your browser, so there is nothing for Nimbuy to store or see.

Processors

  • Cal.com for booking
  • Google for calendar, meeting links and email
  • GitHub Pages for hosting

These providers may process data outside your country, including in the United States, under the transfer safeguards their own terms set out.

How long it is kept

Booking records and enquiry emails are kept for up to 24 months after the last contact. If work goes ahead, records are kept until the work and any follow-up end, and then for as long as the law requires business records to be held. Earlier deletion on request.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask for a copy of what is held about you, or ask for it to be corrected, deleted, restricted or moved. Email nimbuygtm@gmail.com and you will get an answer within one month. If you are not satisfied you can complain to your local data protection authority.

About the score

The audit is an independent Nimbuy check of the listing text you enter, using the same fixed word and field checks on every listing. It is not an AWS rating, it is not endorsed by AWS, and it does not predict AWS approval, search placement or sales. The market figures on this site are aggregates from public AWS Marketplace listing data, and describe those listings at the time they were read. No individual listing score is ever published.
